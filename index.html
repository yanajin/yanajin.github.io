Yana Jin 靳雅娜

Assistant Professor · Peking University

Department of Environmental Management
College of Environmental Sciences and Engineering

My research focuses on non-market valuation and the economic analysis of energy and environmental policies. Before joining Peking University, I was a High Meadows Postdoctoral Economist at Environmental Defense Fund and a Mellon Postdoctoral Fellow at William & Mary. I received my Ph.D. in Environmental Economics (2017), B.A. in Economics, and B.S. in Biology (2011) from Peking University.

靳雅娜，北京大学环境科学与工程学院助理教授、博导、研究员，主要从事环境价值评估及能源与环境政策的经济分析。加入北大前，曾在美国环保协会和威廉玛丽学院从事博士后研究。博士毕业于北京大学，并拥有北京大学经济学和生命科学双学士学位。
